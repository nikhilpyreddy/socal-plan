San Diego → Los Angeles

About 115 mi from the Pacific Beach Airbnb to LAX. It takes about 1h 50m with clear roads and 3h+ at rush hour. Since the date is open, pick a departure window that avoids the worst traffic.

🚗 1h 50m–3h+ · 115 mi 🛣 I-5 N → I-405 N
Open route to LAX
Best times to leaveOn a weekday, leave before 6:30 AM or between 9:30 AM and 1:30 PM. Northbound I-5 / I-405 through Orange County is worst from 2:30 to 7 PM.
Option A: Tue Nov 17, after dinnerLeave Gaslamp around 9 PM and reach LA around 11 PM. Roads are clear, but you need a place to stay in LA that night and should skip the last Airbnb night.
Option B: Wed Nov 18, mid-morningCheck out, leave around 9:30 AM and reach LA around 11:45 AM. This is the most relaxed option.
Scenic detour (+45–60 min)Leave I-5 at Dana Point and take the Pacific Coast Highway through Laguna Beach and Crystal Cove for lunch with ocean views, then rejoin the I-405 at Irvine or Costa Mesa.
If you are flying outPlan to reach LAX 2–2.5 h before a domestic flight and 3 h before an international one. Add 30–45 min if you are returning a rental car.
Rest stopThe Aliso Creek rest area on I-5 at Camp Pendleton is the easy halfway break.
